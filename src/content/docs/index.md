**Goal:** a headless, fast, iteration-friendly simulator of the Naruto TCG (currently unreleased), built to collect high-volume data from agent-driven simulations.

Where to look first:

- [Engine research](research/engine-research/): which open-source TCG engines we looked at, and what to borrow.
- Sprints, in the sidebar once `dandori sprint new <nn-name>` has made one: every track, its branch and its state.
- Trackers: allocation, parity and dataflow, generated from the ledgers in `ledgers/`.
- The Glossary, which fills from `ledgers/glossary.json`.

This site is built with Astro Starlight from the `dandori-lite` starter and published with GitHub Pages from `docs/site`.
