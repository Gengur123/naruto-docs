title: Trackers
description: Allocation, parity and dataflow trackers.
---

The three trackers are generated by `dandori tracker render` from the ledgers `allocation.json`, `parity.json` and `dataflow.json` in `ledgers/`. Those ledgers are empty here, so there is nothing to show yet.

Add an entry to a ledger, run `dandori tracker check` to reopen every anchor, then `dandori tracker render` to rewrite the pages in this folder.
