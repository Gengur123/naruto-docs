Nothing is written here yet. This is the place for open questions for the owner, design notes, balance tables and small tools the owner plays with. Everything on these pages is assumed to change, so make each change its own commit: history then shows how the pages drifted.

`dandori delta <folder> --reader <name>` prints what changed in a folder since a reader last looked, and `--mark` records that they have.
